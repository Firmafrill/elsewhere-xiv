What about the graphics update?

Major expac changes such as 6.x, 7.x, and 8.x can make a mess of the guides and resources that are accurate and up to date. Every item on the site is marked for compatibility for the most recent expansion. Want something that definately works in 8.x? Only use a guide or resource marked for 8.x.

FFXIV VERSION INFORMATION.

Final Fantasy XIV changes constantly, and major updates can break a lot of guides, mods, tools, and resources. I am one person, and I cannot promise that everything on Elsewhere will always be up to date but version tags are there to make it clear what expansion a guide or resource was made for.

Only the current and immediately preceding expansion will be shown on the front page by default. When a new expansion releases, the oldest expansion will be moved out of the default search results and can instead be found through the advanced search options. This is simply to keep extremely outdated information from taking priority over newer resources. Please, keep in mind that any guide or resource tagged for older expansions will likely not work in the current state of the game! Please, only use up-to-date resources unless you know what you're doing.

Older resources will not be deleted simply because they are outdated. They will remain archived and accessible for anyone who needs information for an older version, legacy tools, or historical reference.

If something does not have a version tag, it is not dependent on a specific game version.
